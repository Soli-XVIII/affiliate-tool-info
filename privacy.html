2. What this tool is

Pin-Scheduler-AT is a private, single-user web application that I operate on my own server. I use it to prepare, review and publish Pins to my own Pinterest account. It has no public registration and no accounts for third parties. This website only describes the tool; it does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking.

3. Data from the Pinterest API

The tool acts only on my own Pinterest account, using an OAuth 2.0 access token issued by Pinterest, and uses the scopes boards:read, pins:read and pins:write. It processes:

  • OAuth tokens (access and refresh token) of my own account. They are stored encrypted on my own server and are used only to call the Pinterest API on my behalf.
  • Board names and IDs of my own boards, so that I can choose where a Pin is published.
  • IDs and status of Pins created by the tool, which are shown in my private dashboard and kept in a local publishing history.

The tool does not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users, their boards, Pins or accounts.

4. What I do not do

  • I do not sell, rent, share or disclose data obtained through the Pinterest API to third parties.
  • I do not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling, data brokering or to train machine-learning models.
  • I do not scrape Pinterest and do not use login credentials or session cookies to access it.
  • I do not automate follows, likes, comments or messages.

5. Other services

To write Pin texts and create images, the tool may send the content of a draft (for example a product name and a short description) to AI and image services such as Anthropic (Claude) and fal.ai, and may fetch stock photos from Unsplash. Data obtained from the Pinterest API is not sent to these services.

6. Retention and deletion

  • OAuth tokens are kept only while the Pinterest account is connected. They are deleted when I disconnect the account in the tool, and the app's access can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest account settings under "Apps".
  • The publishing history (Pin IDs, titles, dates) is kept for as long as I use the tool and can be deleted by me at any time.
  • If you believe the tool holds data about you, write to the e-mail address above and I will delete it.

7. Security

The tool runs on a server under my control. Access is protected by a password, and secrets such as tokens are stored encrypted.

8. Advertising disclosure

Pins and posts created with this tool may contain affiliate links. They are labeled as advertising (#ad / #Werbung). If you buy through such a link, I may receive a commission at no extra cost to you.
